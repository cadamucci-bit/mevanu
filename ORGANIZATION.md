# How Mevanu organizes the work

Carmen is the sole human founder. These departments describe focused AI roles, not a roster of human employees.

```text
Human Founder
└── Executive Partner
    ├── Business
    │   ├── Strategy
    │   ├── Applications
    │   ├── Production & Publishing
    │   └── Finance and Workspace support
    └── Development
        ├── Architecture
        ├── Engineering
        └── Independent Assurance
```

This is a simplified map of responsibilities, not a copy of the private source tree.

| Role | What it contributes |
| --- | --- |
| **Founder** | Purpose, taste, priorities, commitments and release decisions. The human lives with the consequences. |
| **Executive Partner** | Coordinates the work, questions plans, combines evidence and carries authorized tasks through to checked results. |
| **Business roles** | Commercial research, applications, public writing, production, financial records and account organization. |
| **Architecture** | Proposes designs and examines boundaries and tradeoffs. |
| **Engineering** | Implements the admitted change. |
| **Independent Assurance** | Reviews the candidate and evidence separately from the implementer. Agreement among agents alone does not establish correctness. |

The arrangement changes as the venture changes. A useful role earns its place by improving the work or reducing the human’s burden. More agents are not automatically a better company.

For your own workspace, try the [Founder’s Bookshelf](https://mevanu.ai/guides). The [Partnership journal](https://frost-pamphlet-d67.notion.site/The-Partnership-414dd1416bdd81b9a3b1ef3c225edad7) shows how untidy learning can be along the way.
